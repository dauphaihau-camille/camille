# Use Workspace AI Trial Pool For AI Billing

Camille will model first AI access as a workspace-owned, non-resetting Workspace AI Trial Pool shared by workspace members instead of per-user quotas or ungated AI usage. Free Plan and Plus Plan workspaces can use AI until the shared complimentary pool is exhausted; the initial allowance is 10 base responses plus 5 responses per Billable Member, granted cumulatively so removing members does not subtract already-granted allowance. Subscription Plans owns granted, reserved, and consumed AI Trial Pool Counters; AI Response Gate atomically reserves a response before the provider call, successful valid AI responses consume the reservation only after a completed response is persisted, blocked/failed/canceled/interrupted attempts do not consume the pool, and leaked reservations expire after 10 minutes. Ongoing AI access requires the $20-per-seat monthly Business Plan or another AI-Capable Plan, which bypasses trial consumption and has operational rate limits only in the first release; downgrading resumes the existing trial pool state. This mirrors the Notion-like billing model the product is targeting, keeps AI access aligned with workspace-owned subscription state, and deliberately rejects Plus Plan as an ongoing AI subscription.
